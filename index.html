The Arrival

He came for the waves. Stayed for the weekend.

Nobody saw the landing. Somewhere off the Gold Coast at first light the water went glassy, the sky went gold, and something tall and impeccably dressed walked out of the swell like it had a reservation.

Saturday is what he left behind: a label for anyone who would rather be remembered than recognised. Pressed, never stressed. Front row, never front desk.

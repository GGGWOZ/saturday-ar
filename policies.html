Shipping

We ship from the Gold Coast, Australia. Delivery options, costs and estimated dispatch times will be shown at checkout before you pay.

Kit-bag sets may arrive in more than one parcel.

Returns, refunds and your rights

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. If something has a major problem (for example it’s faulty, not as described, or not what you ordered), you’re entitled to a refund or replacement. For minor problems, we’ll repair or replace it.

Change-of-mind returns: to be confirmed before checkout opens.

To raise a problem with an order, contact us (details below) with your order number and a photo if it’s faulty.

Size notes

The tees are unisex, sized S, M, L, XL and XXL, with a relaxed, boxy fit. Size up for an oversized look. A measured size chart will be added before checkout opens.

Product images

Product images are mockups, not photos of finished products. Final colours and print details may differ slightly.

Privacy

Email list: if you join the list, your email address is encrypted in your browser before it’s sent, and only Saturday for the Win can read it. We use it only to tell you about Saturday drops, and you can ask to be removed at any time.

Cart: your cart is stored in your own browser (local storage) and isn’t sent to us.

Checkout: when checkout opens, payment and delivery details are collected by the payment provider (for example Shopify or Stripe) under their privacy policy. This site never sees your card details.

Contact

Contact details will be added here before checkout opens.

Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST.

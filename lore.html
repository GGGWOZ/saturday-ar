File 001 · The Saturday Alien

Alley.

There is a seam on the horizon where the surf meets the sky. Most people never see it. Alley lives on it.

Alley is the Saturday alien. Came down off the Gold Coast one glassy morning, paddled out before anyone was awake and never went home. Rides snow at dawn, surf at dusk, and reads a swell like a star chart.

The gold ring isn't decoration. It's a homing beacon. Wherever it tilts is where the next clean line is.

  • Never takes a bad wave.
  • Never seen in the same place twice.
  • Answers every signal. (Tap the head anyway.)
File 002 · Signal 001

Drop 001.

Saturday doesn't count seasons. It counts signals. Every drop is a transmission Alley beams home from the seam where the surf meets the sky. This is the first one: Signal 001.

The first transmission came back with company. The Agent walked out of the swell with a board under one arm and a gold mic in the other, and he hasn't checked out since. Shades on, bow tie straight, cover never blown.

Drop 001 is what Signal 001 carried: free wallpapers, stickers and a QR that summons the hologram. Take what you like. Signal 002 is already somewhere past the ring.

File 003 · Signals

The codes.

Every Saturday tee and sticker carries a code. To most people it's a square of dots. To Alley it's a signal flare. Each one is a tiny transmitter tuned to the gold ring: the drops are Alley's signals home, and a scan is yours back.

Scan one and Alley picks it up, wherever he is, and comes to see who called. The big gold code on the 001 QR Tee is the loudest signal of the lot: you can scan it from across the car park.

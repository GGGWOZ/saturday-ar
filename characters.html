Meet the Crew · The Roster

The inventors of the game.

Every sport started somewhere. Legend says ours started with three: the king who invented games, the captain who made the first tracks in the snow, and the champion who rides both ends of the infinity loop, from the surf to the summit. One-of-a-kind, and every one of them started on a Saturday.

01 · Surf & ski · Drop 001

Alley.

01The Tide-Glider · Champion of Both Tides · Nos. 1–100

The surf-and-ski legend. Rides snow at dawn, surf at dusk, and never picked a side, so he won both.

A sleek navy star-kin in a sharp tuxedo, with gold-rimmed eyes, glowing teal antenna tips and a flat, board-shaped tail. His gold halo is a champion’s ring now, and it carries the Halo Medal: a wave flowing into a snow peak, the same journey as the Saturday loop. His signature move is the Double Tide: carve a wave, launch, spin once through his own halo, and land on fresh powder on the other side of the mountain. On Saturday nights the shades go on, the bow tie gets straightened, and the locals call him the Agent. He doesn’t correct them.

Legend says

Alley followed a single word across the sky (Saturday) to a seam off the Gold Coast, and rode the first wave that ever reached the snowline. He never decided which tide he liked better, so he won both. That’s why he wears the medal on his halo and the tux to every final. Scan a Saturday code and he’ll run out to see who called. “Mic check: Saturday?” (The crowd: “Always.”)

  • PositionTide-Glider: surf and ski, both ends of the loop
  • Signature moveThe Double Tide
  • BadgeThe Halo Medal
  • Says“Why pick a tide?”

02 · Ski & snowboard · Drop 001

Admiral E. Byrd.

02The Captain · First Tracks · Nos. 101–200

The explorer-athlete who made the first tracks: inventor of skiing, and (one winter later) snowboarding.

A frost-white snow lizard with teal frill fins, molten-gold eyes and a scar over one eye he won’t explain. Weathered navy admiral’s cap with the First Tracks Compass badge, aurora-teal goggles pushed up, a teal captain’s armband and long vintage wooden skis. First chair, fresh tracks, and the tea’s on afterwards. The “E.”? He’s never said.

Legend says

The Admiral flew too far south and got gloriously lost. Under the aurora he found a hidden camp of Alley’s star-kin, gliding down the ice on polished boards. He came home with a scar, a compass that points to fresh snow, and the first pair of skis. The next winter he snapped one in half, stood on it sideways, and invented snowboarding. When Alley’s signal reached his brass radio, he finished his tea and answered: “Signal received. Kettle’s on.” That’s why Saturday runs from surf to snow.

  • PositionCaptain: ski and snowboard
  • Signature moveFirst Tracks (and “the Plank”)
  • BadgeThe First Tracks Compass
  • Says“Someone had to go first.”

03 · The First Games · Drop 001

King G.

03Father of Games · Nos. 201–300

The father of games and founder of the First Games. The oldest legend on the roster, and he still wears the highest numbers, because the founder always signs last.

An elephant glyph king with smooth teal-slate skin, big fin ears glowing with a crossword-grid of glyphs, gold-capped tusks, a squared braided beard bound in gold and a single gold halo. His crown is the Rook Cup Crown: chess-rook battlements, a chessboard band and two trophy handles, because the crown and the cup were always the same thing. After every Games he takes it off and lifts it over his head. That’s the Crown Lift.

Legend says

Long before anyone kept score, older even than the tales of Atlantis, King G drew the first square in the sand and called it a board. Then came the first race, then the long run (the marathon), then the First Games. As the story goes, every game since, from chess to the crossword, is “one of King G’s”. (The real credit belongs to brilliant people all over the world. Just don’t tell him.) He napped a few thousand years under the jungle until two signals crossed overhead and his glyphs lit up teal. “…Saturday? Game on.”

  • PositionFather of Games, king of the court
  • Signature moveThe Crown Lift
  • BadgeThe Rook Cup Crown
  • Says“Every game is one of mine.”

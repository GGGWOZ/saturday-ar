Alley.

The Saturday alien. Rides snow at dawn, surf at dusk, steers by a gold ring, and never takes a bad wave.

A sleek navy star-kin alien in a sharp tux, with gold-rimmed eyes, teal-tipped antennae, a floating gold ring and a flat, board-shaped tail. The ocean gave him a few otter habits: he floats on his back between sets and holds onto a mate so nobody drifts off. The locals call him the otter in the tux. He doesn’t correct them.

Backstory

Alley followed a single word across the sky (Saturday) and it led him to a seam off the Gold Coast. He paddled out one glassy morning and never went home. The gold ring isn’t decoration: it’s a homing beacon that finds the next clean line, and it’s how he hears you. Scan a Saturday code and he’ll come and see who called.

  • TurfGold Coast point breaks at first light
  • StyleCalm, few words, always dressed for it
  • Says“Paddle out. Look up.”

The Agent.

The first transmission came back in a dinner jacket and never checked out.

Shades on, bow tie straight, gold mic in one hand and a board under the other arm. The Agent is the loud one: Saturday nights, beach bonfires, the gig after the session. In the hologram he’s the Frontman.

Backstory

When Alley sent Signal 001, the reply didn’t come back as static. It came back with company. The Agent walked out of the swell with a board and a gold mic, and his cover has never been blown. Alley keeps it quiet; the Agent keeps it loud. “Mic check: Saturday?” (The crowd: “Always.”)

  • TurfThe stage, the bonfire, the after-party
  • StyleStrut first, soundcheck later
  • Says“Front row, never front desk.”

Admiral E. Bird.

The world’s greatest camper: a villain’s tailoring, a mastermind’s purr and the heart of a mountain-rescue volunteer.

A frost-white snow lizard with an icy, teal-edged frill (it flares when he’s pleased with himself), gold eyes, a monocle, a navy peaked cap with a gold ring-and-snowflake badge and a long navy expedition parka. Brass kettle and coiled rope, always. The “E.”? He’s never said.

Backstory

When Alley’s first signal bounced around the planet, one brass radio picked it up, in an igloo at the bottom of the world. The Admiral finished his tea, then answered: “Signal received. Kettle’s on.” That reply is Signal 002, and it’s why Saturday goes from surf to snow. He runs Camp Beyond, the finest campsite past the ice, and he’ll tell you he captured no one. He hosted them.

  • TurfFirst chair, fresh tracks, après done properly
  • StyleGentleman villain, actually the hero
  • Says“Sit. You look like you need soup.”

King Ozzie.

A 10,000-year-old alien king of lost Atlantis who never stopped being a dude.

An Atlantean star-kin with glowing teal eyes and glyph tattoos, big fin-shaped ears, a short trunk, gold-capped signal horns, a braided beard bound in gold rings, a floating gold halo and the Tide Key: a gold staff topped with a ring holding a surf fin. Hemp explorer shirt, cargo shorts, zero chill.

Backstory

Long before Saturday was a weekend, it was Atlantis’s day off: every seventh day the ring-city stopped work, surfed the harbour and listened to the stars. Ozzie was its young king. When the city sank he carried the Tide Key to the last dry corner of the Ring and “rested his eyes” under the jungle for a few thousand years, until two signals crossed overhead and his tattoos lit up teal. “…Saturday?” Now he’s after the Endless Saturday.

  • TurfRainforest, waterfalls, river canyons, road trips
  • StyleLoud, fearless, big-hearted, accident-prone
  • Says“Not lost. Exploring.”

Buzz.

A palm-sized gold beetle drone with teal wings, and opinions.

Buzz was built to polish Atlantis’s tall gold Signal Pylons. He scouts ruins, projects hologram maps and sulks when ignored (wings go grey, faces the wall). He is convinced the Admiral’s kettle is a rival drone.

Backstory

When the city sank, Buzz folded his teal wings beside his King and powered down. Signal 003 woke him first, and he woke Ozzie, mostly by bonking him on the trunk. Scout, hologram projector, professional sulker. Never ignore Buzz.

  • TurfWherever Ozzie is, about a metre ahead
  • StyleLoyal, nosy, dramatic
  • Says“Bzzt.” (It means a lot.)

The world

The Saturday Ring.

Long ago, a sea-loving people from past the stars (the star-kin) gave a ring-shaped island city a gold ring that let the sea and the sky talk. That city was Atlantis, and every seventh day it downed tools, surfed the harbour and listened to the stars. They called it Saturday. The city sank; the day off survived. Now the signals are coming back, one drop at a time.

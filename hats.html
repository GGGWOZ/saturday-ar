About the codes

Every Saturday piece links back to Alley, but a QR code only scans when it is printed sharp. Stitched codes blur, so the embroidered hats carry Alley and the Signal mark instead, and the Holo Bucket Hat carries the scannable code, printed. It opens Scan to Reveal.

Delivery

Printed hats dispatch in 3–5 business days; embroidered hats dispatch within 3 weeks, and some take 2–3 weeks to arrive. Prices are planned in Australian dollars (GST included) and may change before launch.
